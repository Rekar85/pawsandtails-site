Everything your pet's care needs, in one app

Built for real pet life: more than one pet, more than one clinic, a sitter while you're away. It's the record you own, so it works with whichever vet you see.

  • Every pet, one app

    Add all your dogs and cats, each with their own profile, reminders and records. Switch between them in a tap.

  • Recurring care reminders

    Set flea and tick, deworming, meds and boosters once. Get a push notification on the day, and a day, three days or a week before if you like. Mark it done and the next one is scheduled.

  • Vaccination log

    Record the date given and the next dose, with a photo of the vaccine sticker. See what's coming up, which helps when you're planning a trip.

  • Documents in one place

    Keep lab results, prescriptions and visit reports from your camera, photos or files, and find them again in seconds.

  • Quick symptom log

    Note what you noticed in a couple of taps, with the time added for you. Show your vet a clear timeline. It records, it doesn't diagnose.

  • Share a read-only summary

    Send vets, sitters and co-owners a one-page summary as a PDF or a link you can turn off any time. They can view it, not edit it, and don't need an account.

Works everywhere you are

Use it on your iPhone, your Android phone or on the web. Your pets and records stay in sync across all of them.
