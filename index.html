02 · Record

Every vaccine, file and symptom. Found in seconds.

  • Vaccination log

    Record the date given and the next dose, with a photo of the vaccine sticker. See what's coming up, which helps when you're planning a trip.

  • Documents in one place

    Keep lab results, prescriptions and visit reports as PDFs or images, from your camera, photos or files.

  • Quick symptom log

    Note what you noticed in a couple of taps, with the time added for you and an optional photo. Show your vet a clear timeline. It records, it doesn't diagnose.

03 · Share

Hand over a summary, not a shoebox.

  • A read-only link you control

    Send vets, sitters and co-owners a link to one pet's summary. Choose what it shows, turn it off any time, and it expires on its own. They can view it, not edit it, and don't need an account.

  • A one-page PDF

    Prefer a file? Make a PDF summary and send it yourself, or print it for the waiting room.
